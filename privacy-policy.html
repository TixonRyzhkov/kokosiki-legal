Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 11, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kokosiki ("Kokosiki", "we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the Kokosiki mobile game (the "App").

1. No account required

Kokosiki does not require you to create an account or provide your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or precise location in order to play.

2. Services used by the App

The App uses third-party services that may automatically process technical, device, advertising, analytics, purchase, and usage information. The services used in the current version may include:

  • Google AdMob — advertising and ad measurement.
  • Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase — analytics and measurement of app usage.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — crash reporting and diagnostics when Crashlytics is enabled in a published version of the App.
  • Google Play Billing — processing and validating in-app purchases made through Google Play.

The exact information processed depends on the SDK version, configuration, device settings, and features enabled in the published version of the App.

3. Information that may be collected

Depending on the services and configuration, the App and its service providers may process:

  • device and app information, such as device model, operating-system version, app version, language, and similar technical information;
  • app-instance identifiers, advertising identifiers, and other device or app identifiers;
  • IP address and approximate location derived from an IP address;
  • information about app launches, sessions, screen or gameplay events, and other usage of the App;
  • advertising information, such as ad impressions, interactions, and related measurement data;
  • information about in-app purchases, such as product identifiers, purchase events, and transaction-related information needed to provide and verify purchases;
  • diagnostic or performance information when such information is collected by the enabled Google/Firebase services;
  • crash-related information, such as stack traces, relevant application state, device metadata, and a Crashlytics installation identifier, when Firebase Crashlytics is enabled.

Google states that Google Analytics for Firebase may collect information such as users and sessions, session duration, operating system, device model, geography, app launches, updates, and in-app purchases.

4. Advertising

Kokosiki uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob and related Google services may process device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about ads and interactions for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

Depending on your device, account, consent choices, and applicable settings, advertising may be personalized or non-personalized. You can manage available advertising controls through your Android device and Google account.

More information is available in Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies information.

5. Analytics

Firebase and Google Analytics for Firebase help us understand how players use Kokosiki, measure game performance, and improve the App. Analytics may automatically collect technical information and events such as sessions, app opens, usage events, and in-app purchase events.

Google provides configuration options for controlling or disabling Analytics collection in an Android app, including Unity-based Android apps.

6. Crash reporting

When Firebase Crashlytics is enabled in the App, it may automatically collect crash-related information to help us identify, diagnose, and fix crashes and other stability problems. This may include stack traces, relevant application state, device and operating-system metadata, and a Crashlytics installation identifier. If we configure custom keys, logs, user identifiers, or non-fatal events, those items may also be included in crash reports.

Crashlytics may also use certain analytics breadcrumb information when Firebase Google Analytics is enabled, which can help show the sequence of app actions before a crash. We intend to use Crashlytics for reliability and debugging purposes, not to collect sensitive personal information.

6. In-app purchases

Kokosiki offers optional in-app purchases through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google Play. We do not receive or store your full payment-card number through the App. The App may receive or process purchase-related information needed to unlock purchased content and verify the transaction, such as a product identifier, purchase status, and transaction-related identifiers.

Google Analytics may also collect in-app purchase events and related parameters for measurement when Analytics is enabled.

7. How information is used

  • to operate and maintain Kokosiki;
  • to provide and verify optional in-app purchases;
  • to display and measure advertising;
  • to understand usage and improve gameplay, features, stability, and performance;
  • to detect abuse, fraud, or technical problems;
  • to comply with applicable legal obligations.

8. Sharing with service providers

Information may be processed by Google and its services, including AdMob, Firebase, Google Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Google Play, as necessary to provide the functions described above. These providers process information under their own privacy policies and terms.

Google Play requires developers to accurately disclose data collection and sharing practices, including practices resulting from third-party SDKs. Our Google Play Data Safety information is intended to be consistent with this Privacy Policy.

9. Data retention and deletion

Kokosiki does not maintain a user account profile. Data collected by Google services may be retained according to the respective Google service policies, settings, and applicable law.

If you believe we directly control personal information associated with you and want to request its deletion, contact us at tixonwork@gmail.com. Requests concerning data controlled directly by Google may need to be submitted to Google through the applicable Google privacy tools or policies.

10. Children

Kokosiki is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us.

11. Security

Google states that data collected by Google Analytics for Firebase is encrypted in transit using TLS. We also take reasonable measures to protect information under our control. No method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure.

12. Your choices

You can manage available advertising and privacy controls through your Android device and Google account. You can also stop using or uninstall the App at any time.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App, its services, or applicable legal requirements change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at tixonwork@gmail.com.
