Data & Deletion

Last updated: September 11, 2026

1. Accounts

Kokosiki does not require users to create an account. There is therefore no Kokosiki account profile that you need to delete.

2. Requesting deletion

If you believe that Kokosiki or a service operated by us holds personal information associated with you, you can request deletion by contacting:

Request data deletion

Email: tixonwork@gmail.com

Please describe the request clearly. We may need enough information to understand and process the request, but please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

3. Third-party services

Kokosiki may use third-party advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services, including Firebase Crashlytics when enabled. Those providers may independently retain certain technical or advertising data under their own policies. Requests concerning data held directly by a third-party provider may need to be made to that provider.

4. What may remain

Information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, fraud-prevention or other legitimate purposes permitted by law.

5. Contact

For questions about deletion or privacy, contact tixonwork@gmail.com.
